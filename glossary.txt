GLOSSARY — Tales of Destiny 2
Aselia-aligned revision for the English fan-translation project

SOURCE AND STATUS POLICY

This game has no official full English localization. Aselia Wiki is used here as the
primary community reference for established English names and terminology, but it is
not an official source.

Status labels:
  [LOCK]              Use this spelling/translation everywhere.
  [ASELIA STANDARD]   Established Aselia Wiki spelling; use as the project default.
  [PROVISIONAL]       Aselia translation from Japanese-only material; may be revised.
  [SCRIPT CONFIRMED]  Japanese term was found in the extracted game script.
  [TEAM DECISION]     A real ambiguity remains; choose once and apply consistently.
  [DO NOT IMPORT]     Term belongs to another Tales game/version and should not be
                      inserted into Tales of Destiny 2 without direct script evidence.

Priority when sources disagree:
  1. Actual Japanese text and control codes in the extracted game files
  2. Explicit project-wide terminology decisions in this glossary
  3. Established Aselia Wiki spelling
  4. Literal romanization or individual translator preference

STYLE RULES

[LOCK] Use "arte", not "art", for battle abilities.
[LOCK] Use "Mystic Arte" only for 秘奥義 (hi-ougi).
[LOCK] Do not translate plain 奥義 (ougi) as "Mystic Arte"; use "Arcane Arte".
[LOCK] Preserve exact capitalization of proper names and menu labels.
[LOCK] Do not confuse Tales of Destiny 2 with the North American title
       Tales of Destiny II, which is Tales of Eternia under a different title.

PLAYABLE CHARACTERS
===============================================================================

[LOCK] カイル・デュナミス (Kairu Dyunamisu) -> Kyle Dunamis
  Protagonist; son of Stahn Aileron and Rutee Katrea.

[LOCK] リアラ (Riara) -> Reala
  Heroine who emerges from a giant Lens in the Laguna Ruins.

[LOCK] ロニ・デュナミス (Roni Dyunamisu) -> Loni Dunamis
  Raised in the Dunamis Orphanage; older-brother figure to Kyle; member of the
  Order of Atamoni.
  Weapon category: Polearm. His usual weapon is described as a poleaxe.

[LOCK] ジューダス (Juudasu) -> Judas
  Masked swordsman; true identity is Lion Magnus.
  Do not write "Judah" or "Judas Magnus".

[LOCK] ナナリー・フレッチ (Nanarii Furetchi) -> Nanaly Fletcher
  Aselia's primary page spelling is "Nanaly Fletcher".
  "Nanaly Fletch" appears in some officially localized crossover material, but this
  project standardizes on Fletcher to avoid mixed naming.

[LOCK] ハロルド・ベルセリオス (Harorudo Beruseriosu) -> Harold Berselius
  Female scientist and creator of the Swordians; younger twin sister of Karell
  Berselius. The masculine given name is intentional.

MAJOR ANTAGONISTS AND DEITIES
===============================================================================

[LOCK] バルバトス・ゲーティア (Barubatosu Geetia) -> Barbatos Goetia
  Major antagonist from the Aeth'er Wars era.
  Weapon: battleaxe.

[LOCK] エルレイン (Erurein) -> Elrane
  Holy Woman and head of the Order of Atamoni; worshiped as a living manifestation
  of Fortuna.
  Do not alternate with "Elraine" in translated text.

[LOCK] 聖女 -> Holy Woman
  Title shared by Elrane and Reala (the "two Holy Women").
  Decision by Gandorff (2026-08-17): use Holy Woman, not "Saintess",
  in all translated text.

[LOCK] フォルトゥナ (Forutuna) -> Fortuna
  Final antagonist and deity worshiped in the altered/future era.
  Fortuna is distinct from Atamoni; do not describe them as the same goddess.
  When the script identifies Fortuna's gender, use "goddess," not "god."

[LOCK] アタモニ神 -> Goddess Atamoni
  Use "Goddess Atamoni" when the title is required and "Atamoni" when only the
  proper name is needed. Do not describe Atamoni as a male god.

[ASELIA STANDARD] ミクトラン (Mikutoran) -> Kronos
  Ancient Aeth'er ruler associated with Dycroft and the Swordian Berselius.
  "Miktran" is a literal/alternate rendering; use Kronos for continuity with Aselia
  and established English Tales terminology.

RACES, HISTORY, TECHNOLOGY, AND CORE LORE
===============================================================================

[LOCK] 天上人 -> Aeth'er
  Formal name of the people who lived on the Aethersphere.
  "Aetherian" may be used naturally as an adjective or for an individual, but use
  Aeth'er in formal race and historical labels.

[LOCK] 地上人 -> Er'ther
  Formal name of the surface people.
  Do not alternate with "E'rther".

[LOCK] 天地戦争 -> Aeth'er Wars
  Ancient conflict between the Aeth'er and Er'ther peoples.

[LOCK] 外殻 -> Aethersphere
  Floating landmass/civilization created with Lens technology.

[LOCK] Er'ther Lands
  Aselia's name for the surface world/planetary lands.

[LOCK] ダイクロフト -> Dycroft
  Capital aeropolis of the Aethersphere.

[LOCK] レンズ -> Lens
  Core magical and technological energy source of the setting.
  Capitalize when referring to the setting-specific substance/artifact class.

[LOCK] 神の眼 (Kami no Me; literal "Eye of God") -> Eye of Atamoni
  Extremely powerful Lens and major artifact.
  Use Eye of Atamoni as the proper name; do not alternate with Eye of God in normal
  translated prose unless the dialogue explicitly explains the literal phrase.

[LOCK] ベルクラント -> Belcrant
  Sword-shaped superweapon powered through Aeth'er technology and the Eye of Atamoni.

[LOCK] ソーディアン -> Swordian
  Sentient Lens-based sword containing a copied human personality.

[LOCK] ソーディアンマスター -> Swordian Master
  Person capable of wielding and communicating with a Swordian.

[LOCK] ラディスロウ / source spelling variants -> Radisrol
  Er'ther command vessel/base associated with the Aeth'er Wars.

[LOCK] 飛行竜 / flying craft context -> Flying Dragon
  Use only where the Japanese identifies this vehicle.

[LOCK] ルミナ・ドラコニス -> Lumina Draconis
  Airship name used in established English Tales terminology.

===============================================================================
ORGANIZATIONS AND RELIGIONS
===============================================================================

[LOCK] アタモニ神団 / matching organization references -> Order of Atamoni
  Formal project term for the religious organization.
  "Church of Atamoni" may be used only when the Japanese wording or ordinary prose
  specifically calls for "church"; do not randomly alternate the organization name.

[LOCK] Fortuna's formal following -> Sacred Order of Fortuna
  "Church of Fortuna" may be used generically when the Japanese wording calls for it.

[LOCK] オベロン社 -> Oberon Corporation
  Powerful corporation central to Tales of Destiny's setting.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
  Atamoni and Fortuna are separate religious figures.
  Elrane is associated with the Order of Atamoni while presenting herself as a
  manifestation/agent of Fortuna.

BATTLE SYSTEM, UI, AND GAMEPLAY TERMS
===============================================================================

[LOCK] トラスト＆タクティカルリニアモーションバトルシステム
  -> Trust and Tactical Linear Motion Battle System (TT-LMBS)

[LOCK] Spirits System
  Battle system governing the characters' spirit gauge.

[LOCK] SP -> Spirit Points (SP)
  SP is spent by actions and restored through defensive/inactive behavior.

[LOCK] TP -> TP
  Tales of Destiny 2 uses TP and SP at the same time.
  Do not state that SP replaces TP.

[LOCK] Spirit Blaster Mode
  Battle state used as a requirement or substitute for the full SP cost of several
  Mystic Artes.

[LOCK] Grade
[LOCK] Grade Shop
[LOCK] Field of Effect
[LOCK] Enchant
[LOCK] Skill Enchant
[LOCK] Monster Book
[LOCK] Strategy
[LOCK] Status Effect / Status Effects

[SCRIPT CONFIRMED] リファイン -> Refine
  Use "Refine" as the verb or menu command.
[ASELIA STANDARD] Refining
  Use "Refining" when naming the overall equipment/system mechanic.

[LOCK] ガルド -> Gald
  Standard Tales-series currency.

[LOCK] Loni weapon category -> Polearm
  Use "poleaxe" for his characteristic weapon where a specific weapon description is
  needed. Do not standardize his entire category as "Halberd".

[PROJECT TERM / VERIFY IN UI] Moon Selector
  Key item used to change the directly controlled character.
  Retain unless an extracted item/menu string establishes a different spelling.

[SCRIPT CONFIRMED] スクリーンチャット -> Screen Chat
  Context indicates a viewed/unviewed skit or chat-gallery feature.
  Preserve capitalization consistently. Do not silently rename it "Skit Theater"
  without direct menu evidence.
